Horror Bot

Horror Bot is a private tool used only by the owner of the "Daily Horror Story" YouTube channel and the "Suman Motion" Facebook Page. It publishes original, fictional, AI-assisted horror story videos to the owner's own channel and page.

Privacy Policy

  • Horror Bot does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any viewer or user.
  • It only uses the channel owner's own permission (YouTube upload access) to upload videos to the owner's own channel.
  • Access tokens are stored privately by the owner and are never shared with anyone.
  • Data deletion: Horror Bot keeps no user data. The owner can remove its access at any time at Google Account › Third-party access.

Terms of Service

Horror Bot is not offered to the public. It is used only by its owner for their own channel and page. All stories are fiction. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.
